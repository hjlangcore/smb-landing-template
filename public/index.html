Home and shopfront styling

Rooms and shopfronts, quietly finished.

A neighborhood studio for one space at a time. Plan a living room, a guest suite, or a small shop counter, then style it so the room can be used the same day.

The offer

Four ways to begin

Each starting point covers a single space. Fees are not listed — a working business would add its own written quote.

  1. 01

    Home styling session

    Walk the room, reset the furniture, and choose textiles and lighting that suit how the space is actually used.

  2. 02

    Color and materials

    A paint and finish direction, plus a sample board you can shop locally — or decline piece by piece.

  3. 03

    Shopfront refresh

    The window, the counter, and the small area around the sign, so a shop reads clearly from the sidewalk.

  4. 04

    Seasonal reset

    A lighter pass before a busy season, a listing, or guests. No structural work is implied.

How the work runs

One point of contact, from the first visit to the styling day.

You talk to the same person the whole way. Nothing is booked until there is a written quote.

  1. 01

    Consult

    A conversation in the space about what should stay and what gets in the way.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A short written direction and a quote. You choose what to proceed with.

  3. 03

    Style

    The agreed day. Afterward, a simple note on how to keep the room in shape.

Plain terms, and a template rule. A written quote comes before work. Suggestions can be bought locally or skipped. There is one lead, not a rotating crew on this demo.
